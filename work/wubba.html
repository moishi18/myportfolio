Wubba is an emerging toy brand built for children aged 1–7 and the parents who choose their toys. The challenge was to create a playful identity that sparks curiosity in children while communicating trust, quality, and durability to parents.

Designed by  Ilisha Dabas

Every element from the logo to the packaging and retail experience—grows from one consistent visual language, allowing the brand to scale confidently across physical and digital touchpoints.

Logo Ideation

Logo Concept

Logo Construction

A playful identity built on trust, imagination, and simplicity.

The Wubba wordmark is inspired by soft modular forms commonly found in children's toys. Rounded geometry, bold proportions, and vibrant colours create a friendly and approachable identity while maintaining the clarity and confidence expected by parents. The result is a timeless logo that balances playfulness with premium quality.

The logo is built on a consistent rounded geometry with uniform stroke weight and smooth corner radii. Each letter follows the same visual rules to create harmony and flexibility across every application.

Color Palette

Combination

Process

Brand Expression

The palette is intentionally modular, allowing colours to be mixed freely across packaging, digital interfaces, retail environments, merchandise, and brand communications while maintaining consistency and recognition.

The colour palette was designed to balance two distinct audiences: children who respond to energy and imagination, and parents who value quality and trust. Every colour was selected to contribute to one cohesive visual language rather than functioning as an isolated accent.

Bold, playful colours capture curiosity and movement, while softer neutrals create space and clarity. This balance gives Wubba a contemporary identity that feels joyful, approachable, and confidently premium.

Solution

Task

The Wubba Custom logotype is built from soft, rounded geometry inspired by playful toy forms. Its organic letterforms create a distinctive and memorable identity while reflecting the warmth, creativity, and friendliness at the heart of the brand. To complement the expressive logo, DM Sans was selected as the supporting typeface. Its clean, contemporary forms provide excellent readability across print and digital applications, bringing clarity, structure, and consistency to the overall identity. Together, the custom wordmark and DM Sans create a typography system that feels playful, approachable, and confidently premium.
