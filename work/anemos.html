Anemos

A symphony of innovation and emotion, Anemos reimagines the bond between machine and movement in the age of electrification. It is not just a vehicle—it is an idea: of sustainability without compromise, of performance without pollution, of silence that speaks power. For those who believe that progress should be beautiful and efficiency should be exhilarating, Anemos is a return—not to the past, but to the purity of purpose. With a sculpted teardrop silhouette born from the wind itself, and a chassis woven from the lightest of materials, Anemos is the art of motion elevated to modern sculpture. Driven by a next-generation dual battery system and optimized for aerodynamic perfection, it delivers up to 1000 km of range, while its intelligent core learns from you, charges with the sun, and returns energy to your home. No engine roar, no emissions—just seamless propulsion and the quiet power of innovation. This is Anemos. Crafted for the conscious driver. Engineered for a world reborn.

From Inspiration to Final Sketch: The Anemos Journey

Tools used for sketching and concept creation:

Inspired by the teardrop forms found in nature and the seamless efficiency of aerospace design, the Anemos sketch evolved through explorations of fluid, low-slung silhouettes and clean surface transitions. Guided by the intent to create a futuristic, aerodynamic EV with minimalist elegance, early sketches prioritized uninterrupted form flow, a flattened underbody for battery integration, and a roofline designed for solar capture. Through iterative refinement, we arrived at a final sketch that balances visual purity with functional innovation—embodying Anemos' identity as a vehicle shaped by wind, light, and purpose.

We chose materials that balance lightness, strength, and sustainability. Aluminum alloys and CFRP reduce weight while maintaining rigidity, while Kevlar and natural fiber composites enhance safety and acoustic insulation. Interior finishes use vegan leather, bamboo, and recycled PET to support eco-conscious luxury.

Inspired by teardrop forms and aerospace flow, the vehicle’s silhouette was shaped for ultra-low drag. CFD testing guided the design of a smooth underbody, tapered tail, and integrated aero elements, achieving a drag coefficient of ~0.20 for maximum range and stability.

Anemos supports ultra-fast 350 kW DC charging, allowing 10–80% top-up in under 30 minutes. Battery architecture and thermal systems were optimized for safe, high-speed energy intake, making long-distance EV travel seamless and efficient.

High-density lithium-ion cells with silicon anodes were selected for their energy efficiency and solid-state readiness. A dual-pack setup—modular underfloor and secondary rear—ensures optimal weight distribution, long range, and supports smart features like swappability and AI-managed thermal regulation.

Research focused on achieving a real-world range of 950–1000 km. By combining aerodynamic design, regenerative braking, and lightweight materials, Anemos meets long-distance needs without compromising performance or luxury. The range is optimized for both city and highway use.

We integrated solar roof panels for auxiliary charging, V2G/V2H for energy sharing, and predictive smart charging. These features position Anemos not just as a car, but as an intelligent, connected energy platform for future mobility ecosystems.
