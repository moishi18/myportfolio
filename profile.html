About me

I’m Ilisha, a product designer, builder, and occasional overthinker.

I study Design at DTU and work across products, interfaces, brands, and technology. Along the way, I’ve worked with startups, led design teams, worked with clients, and taken ideas from a rough sketch to something people can actually use.

I also spend a lot of time outside the usual design bubble, working with Team Inferno, exploring robotics and physical computing, experimenting with AI and 3D, and generally finding excuses to build things.

I care about making things clear, useful, and a little unexpected, but I’m just as interested in everything that happens before the final screen: asking better questions, figuring out the messy bits, and getting people moving in the same direction.
