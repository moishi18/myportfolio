Currently into ...

Currently, I’m exploring the space between design and technology experimenting with AI, creative coding, 3D, and physical computing. I’m fascinated by how things work, why people interact with them the way they do, and how small observations can turn into meaningful ideas. Lately, I’ve been especially interested in the process of taking something from a rough concept to a working prototype, whether that means building digital experiences, tinkering with robotics, exploring new tools, or experimenting with interactions that feel a little different.
